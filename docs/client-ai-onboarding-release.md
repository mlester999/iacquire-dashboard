## Client path

1. An agency creates the client workspace and invites its owner. Six tenant-scoped employee records already exist, initially without Hermes profiles or live access.
2. The verified owner saves and resumes the existing adaptive six-stage business questionnaire. Website-derived suggestions and sensitive business answers still require confirmation. The final submission saves the company profile and approved knowledge.
3. The approved platform model proposes nine bounded, role-specific behavior fields for each employee: personality, traits, communication style, response behavior, procedures, proactive responsibilities, reporting, responsibilities and goals. Invalid, risky, identical or unavailable model output falls back to named deterministic role defaults. Confirmed onboarding answers and approved tenant knowledge supply business facts. Identity, tone/language choices, role answers, company facts, escalation rules, approval behavior and tools remain mapped from owner answers or protected defaults; the model cannot provide pricing, policies, services, contact details, permissions or credentials.
4. The existing guarded version path applies safe configurations and holds risky ones for agency review. The compiled request layers client-configurable behavior and business context beneath immutable policy. Each employee has distinct personality, behavior, procedures, responsibilities, reporting and role answers. Missing operational facts stay missing.
5. Six idempotent provisioning requests appear in the client detail queue for an eligible, verified, non-demo client. The restricted worker leases only an applied client configuration on its pinned, verified client-only gateway. It creates a blank profile, never clones an existing employee, and reports a host attestation to the portal. The worker remains disabled until the separate runtime has passed isolation review.
6. On completion, the client sees six persisted states and a real ready count. Chat links appear only after the existing readiness gate accepts an applied configuration, current credential, authenticated identity, complete safe tool inventory and a passing health check. External booking and messaging still require their own verified connections; the optional maintenance plan does not gate core chat.

The Manage AI Employee screen exposes all nine generated behavior fields in a preview. Regeneration produces a preview; applying it to a draft requires an explicit click, and saving follows the guarded version path. Later business updates refresh untouched shared fields and knowledge while preserving recorded manual overrides across repeated submissions.
