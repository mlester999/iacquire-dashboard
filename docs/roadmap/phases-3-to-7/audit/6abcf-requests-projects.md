## Summary
Support & Requests is a mature, well-tested shared request model: migration 0065, src/server/support/* (about 4,100 lines with the projects code), 11 support test files plus tests/support-world fixtures, no skipped or todo tests, and it is already merged (feat/support-requests 332dea1 is an ancestor of HEAD and master). It covers Get Support, Report an Issue, Provide Feedback, My Requests, threads, internal notes (restrictive RLS), attachments, assignment, priority, escalation, link/merge, duplicate hints, rule-based plus on-demand AI help, consent-gated diagnostics, response clocks, de-duplicated notifications, analytics and scheduler jobs. Custom projects (0015, src/server/projects/*) is a separate model (client_project_requests, project_messages, project_quotes) that is solid for catalog price guidance, manual versioned quotes with a content hash and a recorded acceptance, milestones with client approval, private files and handover, but it has no change-request or out-of-scope ledger, no payment/contract prerequisites, no structured warranty or revision tracking, no idempotency, no duplicate detection, no search, no response expectations, no overdue job and no survey. Missing across the area: Request a Change as a Requests Center item, staff @mentions, satisfaction surveys and explicit resolution confirmation, a persisted and reviewable AI triage record with a routing suggestion, and a unified My Requests that includes projects and maintenance. I found one high-severity internal-note leak (the client data export dumps internal_notes of project requests and quotes), one state-machine defect (a cancelled or held project can be re-approved by accepting a stale quote), and several smaller authorization and consistency defects. No other ref carries relevant support, survey, mention or change-request work (checked by migration names, files added versus HEAD and path-filtered commits across all 65 refs); only the real AI provider, real email delivery, the scheduler on a host and hosted RLS remain unverified, and whether 0065 is applied to production cannot be proven from the tree.

## Founder decisions
- Is migration 0065 (Support & Requests) already applied to the hosted database? The tree cannot show it; confirm schema_migrations before any 0090 dry run. Approval for 0090 follows the usual backup, dry run, one-at-a-time rule.
- Request a Change policy: what counts as covered by the core system, covered by the managed-maintenance monthly allowance (the proposed 4 per month still awaits confirmation), or an out-of-scope paid change that must be quoted. Engineering will not invent rates or an hourly price list; change quotes are written by staff only.
- Custom-project revision and warranty policy: how many revisions a project includes by default when the quote is silent, the support/warranty period after handover (days), and whether defects in a delivered custom build are fixed free inside that period or go to a quote or the maintenance plan.
- Payment and contract prerequisites: which items must be confirmed before a custom project starts (signed agreement, deposit, percentage, none). Until Phase 5 chooses providers (PayPal, PayMongo, an e-signature service), these are manual confirmations recorded by a person; nothing is detected or charged.
- Satisfaction surveys: turn them on at all (default off), the delay after resolution, whether individual answers are shown to staff or only aggregates, and any privacy wording; plus whether project completion should also ask for feedback.
- Response expectations for custom projects: whether to set a review target (hours) and show it to clients, as the support targets already can be; support targets themselves remain off until the Super Admin enables them.
- AI triage: approval to enable AI-refined summaries and questions with a real provider (needs a provider key or Hermes provisioning, a small evaluation set, and sign-off on redacting emails, phone numbers and secrets before text leaves the portal). Until then the triage is rules-only and says so.
- Staff mentions: in-app only or also email, and whether mentioned colleagues outside a client's assignment can be mentioned (recommended: only agency people already in scope for that client).
- Whether the unified My Requests should show custom projects and maintenance inside the same list (recommended) or keep them as separate destinations, and whether the sidebar item is called 'Request a Change'.
- Whether the high-severity export leak and the stale-quote fix should ship as a small standalone release ahead of the rest of Phase 6 (recommended); this needs the usual push and deploy approval.
