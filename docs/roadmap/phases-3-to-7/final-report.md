## 10. Security review

A fresh adversarial review of the new surfaces ran this session with two independent read-only reviewers, and is written up in `docs/security-review-2026-10-26.md`. **No Critical finding.** Two High findings were confirmed and fixed:

1. `/api/assets/upload` parsed the request body **before** authenticating, with no byte ceiling; eight sibling upload routes had no ceiling either. Every file or free-text body now goes through the bounded reader, the upload route authenticates first, and `tests/upload-bodies-bounded.test.ts` keeps the list from growing.
2. The per-role tool allowlist was **advisory by default**, so every client profile was provisioned with the public web toolset. It is now enforced unless a deployment deliberately writes `report`, an over-wide profile is refused at the connection check, and the variable is documented in `.env.example` and `.env.staging.example`.

A Medium (the admin client-detail reader that bypassed the D70 switch) is also closed, and a Low deadlock shape in credit-note writes was fixed. Three Low findings are accepted with reasons in that document, including the authenticated JSON endpoints, which are now pinned by a test so the set cannot grow unnoticed.

Also confirmed sound by reading (not by a penetration test): webhook signature verification on both money providers (PayPal by asking PayPal, PayMongo by timing-safe HMAC), replay and idempotency at the database, amount and currency comparison against our own rows with a hold on mismatch, every bearer link's randomness and hash-only storage, the sign-in redirect, the same-site check with `Origin: null`, storage keys and cross-tenant reads, and the health probe's scheduler-only entry point.

**Found in the verification session, all fixed:** (Medium) a PayPal `BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.PAYMENT.FAILED` event was dated by the subscription's `status_update_time`, which only moves when the status moves, so after the first collected renewal every later failure looked older than that charge and was dropped silently; it is now dated by the event, then by the failure PayPal records on the subscription (a regression test fails without the fix). (Low) the AQi Operations copilot showed approval titles and run error text to a Super Admin with no support session even with the D70 rule on; both are now withheld for a client with no session (rule off: unchanged). (Low) a refund or dispute on a PayPal payment was attributed to PayMongo on the admin payments tab, and PayPal's reversals were not listed. (Low, development only) the QA simulator's address could in principle be configured anywhere; it is refused in production, off this machine, in the live environment and for any client id but its own (`tests/paypal-simulator-guard.test.ts`). The edge handler's production block of `/dev/*` is now also proved by running the real handler for every dev route in every method (`tests/dev-routes-production.test.ts`).

## 15. Performance and cost safety

Full audit with method, tables and what was not measured: `docs/performance-cost-reliability-2026-10-26.md`. Everything is on embedded Postgres; the statement counts carry over to a hosted database, the milliseconds do not.

* Builds at this commit: Turbopack 119 s at a 5,902 MB peak; Webpack 345 s at 8,489 MB with the script's 8 GB cap (it ran out of memory once at the default, and passed at 4 GB, 8 GB and the default in other runs: `docs/production-build-and-bundler.md`).
