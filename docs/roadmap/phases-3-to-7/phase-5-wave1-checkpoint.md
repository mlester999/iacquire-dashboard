## 1. What was built

* **Proposals (5A, migration 0091).** A client writes a priced proposal for its own customer. Price book, the workspace's own tax rates and templates (nothing is seeded; a template is wording plus starting price book items, versioned, and the owner approves a version before anyone sends from it). Money is integer minor units (`src/lib/money`), one rounding per line, one-time, monthly and yearly amounts totalled apart. A version is frozen when submitted or sent; an approval is of one exact version bound to a content hash; approval is required for an AI draft always, for an author who cannot send, for a discount or a price below the price book, and above an owner-set total; the two-person rule is optional. Sending freezes the version, stores the PDF (own deterministic writer in `src/lib/pdf`, no dependency; written after the send commits, the page falls back to rendering from the version if that fails) and makes the customer's link. **Sending does not email the customer: the team copies the link.** The customer's page `/proposal/<token>` (plus PDF and `/api/public/proposals/<token>/{view,code,verify,decide}`) uses an emailed one-time code before any answer; an answer is accepted, declined or "changes requested", append-only and bound to the hash. AI drafting names price book items by id and writes a few paragraphs (no field for a price, total, discount, tax, date or terms); every sentence is checked and a failing one is dropped. CRM effects (timeline lines, events, tasks, notices; optional tag, stage and won-stage), seven `proposal.*` Automation Studio triggers, a Proposals tab on contact and deal pages, export, offboarding and erasure hooks. Jobs `proposalExpiry` and `proposalHousekeeping`.
* **E-signature (5B, migration 0092).** A provider seam with one adapter (PandaDoc, the client's own account; **never run against PandaDoc**) and an in-process stand-in service (refused in production). One live request per sent version, locked to the content fingerprint and the PDF's sha256; the intent is written before any call that could make a document; a lost answer is checked with the service and never repeated. A signature counts only from a webhook signed with that connection's own key, or a status read with the same key, and is applied only if the proposal is still sent, on that very version, unanswered and in date; otherwise it is kept and set aside with a plain reason. The signed copy is stored in `proposal-documents`; PandaDoc's certificate is not fetched. Signature tab, connection card on `/proposals/settings`, webhook route `/api/webhooks/esign/<connectionToken>`, job `esignSync`. Withdrawing a PandaDoc request asks the business to withdraw it in PandaDoc (no remembered call). The product never calls a code, a box or a typed name a signature.
* **Customer invoices (5C, migration 0093).** Draft, pending approval, issued, void; approval and issue are one act by someone holding `invoices.approve`; the number is assigned inside the issuing transaction, details and a content hash are frozen at issue, and the database refuses edits to an issued invoice and totals its rows do not add up to. Write-off, manual payment records (a method and evidence required) and refund records, line items, tax, discounts, eight currencies (USD, PHP, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD, JPY; a database check). Print page instead of a PDF. Overdue sweep (`invoiceOverdueSweep`). Reminders through the consent-gated send chain under a new `billing` scope: the owner authorizes the exact wording, days, channels and maximum (a fingerprint; lapses after 90 days or on any change); email only by default; the stored message holds `{{invoice.link}}`, filled only at dispatch (job `invoiceReminders`). Eight CRM events, a read-only `receivables_review` capability for the Performance Analyst. Test invoices (`is_test`, owner-only, `TEST-` numbers). Staff screens `/invoices`, `/invoices/new`, `/invoices/[id]`, `/invoices/[id]/edit`, `/invoices/settings`; customer page `/pay/<token>`.
* **Customer payments and verified revenue (5D).** The client's own PayMongo (PHP only) or PayPal account; iAcquire never holds funds or sees a card. Owner-only connection (`invoices.connect_payments`) after accepting recorded wording (draft wording, not counsel-approved); keys sealed (AES-GCM) in a column the application role cannot read, behind one seam (`credentialVault`). One open checkout per invoice at the server's balance; the provider's return address carries our checkout reference, never the invoice token. Per-tenant webhook route `/api/webhooks/customer-payments/<provider>/<connection key>`; receipt committed, then applied, then marked on error (three transactions); only `src/server/invoices/apply.ts` writes paid, refunded or payment status (a source-scan test enforces it). A payment counts only when mode, reference, checkout id, amount, currency and (where asked) the provider read-back all agree; anything else is kept for a person. The return page `/pay/return/<reference>` says "We are confirming your payment" until the database counts it. Disputes close with an outcome (won restores; lost records a `chargeback` refund; unknown stays disputed for the owner), and the owner can open and close one by hand. A provider refund pairs with the same refund recorded by hand. Go-live needs six gates (connected, owner authorization, `CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS_WRITES_ENABLED`, `CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS_LIVE_ENABLED`, a provider-confirmed test payment, the owner's confirmation); a customer is never sent to a provider's test mode. Job `customerPaymentReconcile`. **Verified revenue** in the Lead Sources report: live, provider-confirmed, received, net of refunds and of tax, per currency, cohort to date with an as-of note; test, simulated and (by default) hand-recorded money excluded, disputed money held out; return on spend only where one cost currency equals a revenue currency. Rules in `src/lib/invoices/revenue.ts` and SQL in `src/server/invoices/revenue.ts`, run over the same random payments by a test that refuses any difference. Settings page `/settings/payments`.
* **Reputation (5F, migrations 0094 and 0095).** Follow-through for low private feedback (an urgent task for the owner, a notice naming the customer and score but not the words, a complaint escalation in the inbox, remaining program messages stopped, a `feedback.escalated` event; the thank-you page differs for low feedback by exactly one sentence). Surveys: CSAT 1 to 5 and NPS 0 to 10 on `/r/<token>`, the Satisfaction tab (a score is shown only above a sample size: 10 and 30, proposals), the retention `{feedback_link}` step, survey forms that save feedback, two Studio triggers (`feedback.escalated`, `review.external_received`), the "Check in after a visit, then ask how it went" template. Review request batches: criteria are a strict object of six keys (no feeling-shaped key can be stored), a set-based preview with exclusion reasons, an owner-only approval bound to a hash (lapses after 14 days), paced 1 to 60 a minute (default 10) through the ordinary consent chain, statuses `paused` and `stopped` in addition to the audit's list (job `reviewRequestBatches`). Rules R1 to R8 (review-gating-safe) are enforced by code and by `tests/reputation-gating-guard.test.ts`. Public reviews (migration 0095): a read-only mirror of Google and Facebook reviews (no method to write, hide or delete a review exists, a test fails if one appears; a reviewer is never matched to a customer by name), replies drafted by a person, approved by version and hash by the owner or a manager, posted only if the words still equal what was approved, the intent written before the call, a lost answer reconciled by reading the site and never resent on a guess; an AI reply assist (off by default, metered, never submits). Jobs `reviewSync`, `feedbackSla`, `appointmentOutcomeNudge`, `renewalReminders`, `reputationRetention`.
* **Retention (5G).** Retention non-customer search fixed (a mangled regex), contact merge reference list fixed (three tables did not exist), erasure of feedback comments and form answers, a person's hold on automatic messages honoured by every automatic review ask and invoice reminder, internal renewal reminders (off by default; a task and a notice, nothing written to the customer).
* **AI cost meter, web chat kill switch and cap, consent rule (cross-cutting, no migration).** `src/server/llm/meter.ts` records every metered model call per token unit in `metered_usage_events` (tagged `ai:<feature>`), charges only where an `llm` unit rate is published (none is, so every line is zero cost), is idempotent per call key and never throws. `WEBCHAT_PUBLIC_DISABLED` and a per-workspace daily AI reply cap (`WEBCHAT_DAILY_AI_REPLIES`, 500 proposed) for the public website chat. The consent rule: an address a website visitor typed and nobody confirmed is not reachable by anything automatic or promotional (`identity_unverified`), a person's one-to-one message still goes.
* **Platform.** Super Admin page `/admin/module-rollout` to switch the six modules on per workspace (each change confirmed, audited, through the module's own service); three more public-page emergency stops (`PROPOSALS_PUBLIC_DISABLED`, `INVOICES_PUBLIC_DISABLED`, `REVIEWS_PUBLIC_DISABLED`); the module documents and the scheduler document, guarded by `tests/docs-scheduler.test.ts` and `tests/docs-modules.test.ts`; top-bar titles for every side-menu entry; shared Input, Select and Textarea at 16 px below the `sm` breakpoint.

## 8. Deviations from the audits

The build stages recorded these; the ones that are really decisions are in `decisions.md` (D32 onward).

* **Proposals.** Migration number 0091 (the audit said 0090, which is the shared buckets). Money columns are `*_minor` bigint (not `*_cents`) and the currency list is the eight codes of `src/lib/money`. `withdrawn` is allowed from any unanswered status. The audit's "AI origin and not draft requires approved_by" is applied only to approved, sent, accepted, declined and expired. `proposal.viewed` fires on the first browser-confirmed view (the beacon), not on the first code verification. A customer's request for changes leaves the proposal `sent` (still acceptable), five a day at most; the audit's separate "staff question" action is not built. The PDF is stored after the send commits. Public pages are unavailable for a suspended, archived or paused workspace. The AI check drops a sentence rather than the whole suggestion and is stricter than the audit (amounts in words, fractions, numbers jammed against a dot). The `/proposals` routes are not in AQi's route registry (it cannot check the workspace switch). No agency screens for the price book and templates (the services accept an agency write session). The approval-over amount is one number compared as a plain number with every currency's total.
* **E-signature.** `esignSync` runs on every tick, not every five minutes (a request would otherwise wait 10 minutes or more; polling at 15 minutes, key checks at six hours and retries keep their own pace). A signature for a request that already ended is kept and set aside (stricter than the committed code). Completing a signature no longer revokes the customer's link. The Signature tab shows only for a proposal that asks for a signature or has had a request. A QA-only "play the signer" action exists only where the stand-in exists. PandaDoc: cancel blocked, certificate not fetched, signature field placement is a guess.
* **Invoices and payments.** Eight notification kinds, not seven. Approval and issue are one act. Write-off uses `invoices.refund` (owner). `balance_minor` and `overpaid_minor` are generated columns. The deal close rule runs after the payment commits and a sweep (inside `customerPaymentReconcile`) repairs an interrupted one. All provider calls, reads included, are gated by `CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS_WRITES_ENABLED` (the audit would allow reads). `fetchCheckout` (checkout plus the payments listed on it) replaces the audit's `fetchPayment`. Webhook deliveries that are not ours are acknowledged and not stored; a refund waiting for its payment is deferred and let go after 72 hours. **Verified revenue does not require the connection to still be live-confirmed** (the audit says it does), and only PayMongo or PayPal can count, never the simulated provider. A test-mode payment starts no event or workflow and never touches a deal. Reminders do not reuse `queueOutreach` (it hard-codes an AI actor). PayPal reconciliation reads orders but does not capture an approved order; only the customer's return page does.
* **Reputation.** `ai_replies_enabled` defaults to false (the audit table said true). The low-feedback notice no longer carries the customer's words (notices can be emailed). Surveys and batches got their own workspace switches (the audit promised "one entitlement seam"). Approval of a batch is a standing authorization row, not the generic approvals queue. The preview is one set-based query kept in step with the per-person check by a parity test. A Facebook connection is created unable to post replies. Reviews with no star rating or recommendation are counted as skipped and not stored. The first read of a connection announces only reviews under 48 hours old. `feedback.escalated` was emitted in C1 and registered as a Studio trigger only in C2 together with its validator rule.
* **AI cost and consent.** No migration was allocated, so usage goes to `metered_usage_events` (zero-cost lines when no rate is published) and the daily cap is a counter in `rate_limit_buckets` (the audit wanted new `usage_events` kinds and `usage_limits` columns, which need a migration). `usage_limits` is not applied to channel replies. The audit's "do not record marketing consent at capture until verified" became "record it with 'Typed by the visitor ... unverified' evidence and ignore a chat-form promotional tick until consent is recorded again". An email address becomes verified by an inbound message only when the receiving server authenticated it. The rule covers contacts created by the website chat only.

## 12. Open risks

From the stage reports and the integrator; none is a known failing test.

* All provider contracts are unproven; a run against each sandbox account may change field names, event names and statuses, and the adapters were written to be read before use.
* A refund pairing (same payment, same amount, within 14 days) is a rule, not a certainty: a cash refund recorded by hand for the same amount as a separate provider refund would pair wrongly and be counted once; there is no un-pair.
* A lost PayPal dispute whose resolution carries no amount stays disputed until the owner records it; PayPal's outcome codes and amount fields are from memory. A chargeback goes through the refund path, so it also announces `payment.refunded` to automations. A dispute close that arrives before its open is deferred and after 72 hours let go with wording that does not fit that case.
* PayPal's scheduled check reads orders but never captures an approved one; a customer who approves and closes the tab leaves an approved, uncaptured order (no money moves). The capture is also stopped by `INVOICES_PUBLIC_DISABLED`. PayPal's 503 behaviour relies on PayPal retrying; its window is a guess.
* Deleting a test invoice deletes the provider-confirmed test payment that lets an owner go live; if the keys were already swapped for live ones the owner cannot repeat the test without swapping back. The dialog warns and offers void.
* `decryptSecret` ignores `APP_ENCRYPTION_KEY_PREVIOUS`: rotating `APP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` makes every sealed credential unreadable (payment keys, signing keys, review-site sign-ins, social tokens, calendar and Automation Studio connections). Documented, not changed, never rehearsed.
* AI checks are nets: the proposal turnaround rule can drop legitimate sentences; the web chat check can over-refuse ("free anytime", "20 pounds" as weight) and misses amounts in words; the price-grounding of `$5k` from a stated `$5` passes.
* Metering: no `llm` unit rate is published, so every metered call is a zero-cost line; a provider answer with no token counts is recorded as a gap; Hermes customer replies still record nothing when no rate is published; the daily cap is a fixed UTC-day window, so a burst can straddle midnight; the metering incident `metering:failed:<feature>` has no collector and never closes itself.
* The wording of D29 (fix brief D9, "every model call checks the workspace first") is wider than what was built: `src/server/support/ai.ts`, `src/server/help/service.ts` and `src/server/messaging/inbox-ai.ts` check neither workspace status nor credit; social, proposal and reply drafting check credit but not status; onboarding assist, the employee generator and the Website Studio assistant check status but not credit (a new workspace has no credit during setup).
* The visitor-typed rule covers only contacts the website chat created. An address typed on a Website Studio form (`src/server/websites/forms.ts`) or by `website_form`, `form`, `booking` or `call` sources is the same kind of fact and is not marked.
* D6 still attributes a survey answer by an address the business has verified, and anyone can type a verified address (accepted by the decision; a per-contact tokenised survey link would close it). Survey answers waiting for a person are low unresolved feedback with no owner until confirmed, so they raise the 24-hour SLA reminder.
* A batch's pace counts every queued, unsent message including customers waiting for the 72-hour retention spacing, so a few such customers make a batch slower, not wrong. A customer who becomes ineligible between the preview and the start is left out silently.
* Removal of a review is withheld while the site reports more reviews than were returned, so a genuinely removed review can stay shown. After a reconnect, reviews that arrived in a gap and are older than 48 hours are announced as new.
* Erasure keeps frozen proposal versions, recorded answers, signing history, issued invoices' numbers, amounts and payments, and each feedback score, pending counsel. Titles are reset only when they contain the person's, their company's or the proposed-to name; typed notes, sections and terms stay and are counted. `proposal_ai_runs` output and timeline lines naming a signer are not scrubbed. Stored PDFs made before the fix keep the full email address until re-made (a re-made PDF has a new sha256).
* Duplicating a proposal without the discount permission strips discounts (a rep cannot copy a manager's discounted proposal as it is).
* Lock order and concurrency are reasoned only. Two Super Admins changing proposals and signing at the same moment can leave signing on without proposals (signing then does nothing); the dependency check is not atomic.
* The signed copy of a completed request on a connection later disconnected can never be fetched (the keys are deleted). A signed PDF over 10 MB cannot be stored.
* `setOrgFeature` in `src/server/proposals/features.ts` does not itself check that the workspace is a client; the rollout page does.
* A suspended workspace's held system program message is re-queued every 5 minutes until the workspace is allowed again (25 looked at per tick).
* About eight call sites outside Wave 1 still force a phone text size under 16 px (agency and admin screens, monospace fields) and will still trigger iOS zoom.
* The shared migration rehearsal ran once after the last migration edit; anything merged later that touches 0087 to 0095 needs it again.
