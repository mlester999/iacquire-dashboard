# AI Growth Partner acquisition source research

**Checked:** 2026-11-04. Provider terms, access, rate limits, and prices change. Recheck the linked primary source and the workspace's own provider console before enabling a source.

## Harvey reference review

The requested [Harvey repository](https://github.com/ethanplusai/harvey) is MIT-licensed. Useful product patterns are a user-confirmed signal catalog, evidence-backed observations, discovery cost estimates, deterministic filtering, a review Outbox, pre-send checks, stop-on-reply and bounce controls, bounded recurring work, and visible failures. Harvey uses several internal agents; iAcquireOS will not reproduce that identity model or copy its Python runtime. This implementation borrows product concepts only, so no Harvey code or dependencies have been copied. If code is later reused, preserve the MIT license and its required notice and attribution.

## UK Food Standards Agency directory

The Food Standards Agency's public Food Hygiene Rating Scheme API v2 provides a free, no-key source for UK food business listings. The adapter uses one HTTPS request to the official `Establishments` search endpoint with `x-api-version: 2`. Its initial scope is the restaurant, café and canteen category and one approved local authority. It uses the authority ID for the provider filter and checks each record against the approved authority code and name. An optional workspace-approved business name keyword filters candidates. [FSA developer guidance](https://api.ratings.food.gov.uk/help) · [FSA endpoint documentation](https://api.ratings.food.gov.uk/Help/Index/)

An owner or admin must confirm the intended use and the licence terms, enter the authority identifiers and request limits, and choose an expiry within 30 days. Approval ends at midnight UTC on the chosen date. Before each call the ledger checks the exact destination and query, current approver, request cap and spend cap. The application allows one page of up to 20 records per job and up to 12 requests per workspace per UTC day, with one in flight at a time and no retry or pagination. These are application limits. Review current service guidance before approving a source run. [FSA open data](https://ratings.food.gov.uk/open-data) · [FSA terms and conditions](https://ratings.food.gov.uk/terms-and-conditions)

The application keeps the business name, category, local authority, public FSA record link and observation time, with Food Standards Agency attribution under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Records with withheld postcodes are excluded. Addresses, contact information, ratings, coordinates, provider comments and supplied URLs are discarded. The directory record does not establish current operations, buyer intent or contact permission. Directory-only records go to human review for first-party evidence. [FSA publication guidance](https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/22046) · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)

Approval is disabled by default. Contract and pipeline tests use fictional directory records. No FSA discovery requests have been made, and live-provider validation is pending separate approval.

## Source activation checklist

Before enabling any external source, record the exact product and endpoint, source purpose, current terms revision and URL, tenant authorization, credentials' tenant binding, numeric rate and concurrency limits, cost model, maximum per-job and daily spend, retention/deletion rules, data fields, provenance, error behavior, and who approved it. If any item is unknown, that source remains disabled. Provider keys, account access, API calls, subscriptions, and paid requests are not authorized by this research note.

## Signal record and execution rules

Every candidate signal must retain the company, source identifier, evidence URL, server-recorded observation time, signal key, extracted evidence, confidence, client-fit explanation, qualification decision, and suggested next action. The system must distinguish a search result from a fact confirmed on the company's own page. Missing, stale, or contradictory evidence remains unknown and routes to review. A score is a research priority, never consent or confirmed interest.

Discovery and contact are separate permissions. A source connection never grants the Growth Partner permission to send a message. Outbound messages, public posts, ads, and campaign launches stay behind the existing specific-action approval path, suppression/opt-out checks, scoped limits, and customer policy. No social platform receives cold automated messages. A source failure, access denial, limit response, or missing budget must stop that source; it must not silently fall back to scraping or another provider.

## Current implementation boundary

The research worker now collects a tenant-approved OSM candidate set and, when configured, reads only matching exact-allowlisted company domains. Tenant policies default to disabled and require an owner/admin confirmation to enable. The Growth Partner control center exposes those settings only to members with the owner/admin research permission, shows request and provider-fee caps separately from currently unmeasured hosting/egress costs, and leaves live endpoints and domains unset until an owner configures them. Saving settings persists the tenant policy and audit record but does not start discovery. A persistent usage row is reserved before each source request and counts against that tenant's per-job and UTC-day request and spend caps, including failed attempts. Evidence is server timestamped and retained with its source label; returned model candidates and citations are bound back to the collected bundle. Source failures stop without fallback.

The first acceptance run used the supplied Orlando metro and four fictional `.test` domains, bundled discovery/HTML fixtures, injected local transports, and a mock Hermes endpoint. The records and results are synthetic. The test socket guard refused all non-loopback connections. The run passed persistence and processing checks with 1 fixture OSM request and 9 fixture website page reads at $0.00 provider fees; it does not establish live discovery, public website access, terms compliance, real Hermes memory, or production performance.

No live OSM or public company website request has passed acceptance. The worker now requires a gateway-advertised per-run `no_tools` capability before the first source request. The Hermes overlay supplies an empty toolset and blocks tool dispatch for a scoped review run; local unit and app-level tests cover this contract. A build and runtime acceptance against the pinned Hermes image is still required before recommending a separate read-only public-source run. That run also needs current endpoint/site terms and robots review, a confirmed hosting/egress budget, and separate owner authorization for external requests. No paid source or outreach is active.

Source settings now report the last successful HTTP response, completed research run with a successful response, and last request error for each current approval. Provider health uses server-recorded request provenance. Rows from fixture transports, older unclassified history and prior approvals are excluded. These are observations of requests already made under an approval. Opening settings makes no provider requests.

## Workspace company lists

Owners and admins can open **Leads → Upload CSV** once the permanent Growth Partner conversation exists. A submission requires the workspace's acknowledgement of its right to use the records. The UTF-8 template accepts up to 100 company rows and 256 KB. Business name, HTTPS website and HTTPS source URL are required. Industry, region and a two-letter country code are optional. The upload omits contact fields and is unavailable in the demo.

The application compares provided fields with the tenant's saved targeting and records exact exclusion matches. It skips same-name or same-domain matches against the tenant's current candidates and CRM companies, tracks the completed intake on the Work Board and reports the source as **Workspace CSV** in Analytics. A supplied link is evidence for the reviewer to check. The upload invokes no inference or source transport and records no observation time or model score. The normal lead review and CRM import approval still apply. CRM import for this source creates or reuses company records and preserves supplied region and country data.

## From discovery to CRM tasks

Saved exact exclusions are applied to approved source fields before company-site reads and qualification. The research report records the count that matched. OpenStreetMap responses are filtered again against the approved coordinates, and FSA results must match the approved local authority and business category. Industry fit, intent and other criteria that depend on incomplete or free-text evidence still require qualification and human review. Source adapters collect evidence only from the approved provider and destination. [Overpass query and geometry reference](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/OverpassQL) · [FSA endpoint documentation](https://api.ratings.food.gov.uk/Help/Index)

When an approved map or directory result needs more evidence, an owner or admin can open it from **Leads → Review queue** and choose **Add first-party evidence**. The reviewer supplies a public HTTPS company page, records what it shows and confirms that they checked it. The application saves a reviewer-supplied citation alongside the original listing, audits the actor, and rechecks the business name and supplied domain against current records in that workspace. It does not fetch the page, run inference, accept contact details, or grant consent. Only an eligible reviewed lead can be submitted for the separate CRM import approval. After that import completes, **Open CRM tasks** on the lead opens the existing task form and task history for its company. A workspace member with task permissions can prepare an internal follow-up there. Analytics groups approved discoveries by original provider and labels fixture rows separately.
