## Implemented and verified locally

- New client workspaces provision one `growth_partner` runtime and one linked Growth Partner identity. Legacy employee records, history, schedules, and credentials are preserved; no existing tenant is silently migrated.

- Client legacy-role chat URLs now redirect into the permanent Growth Partner conversation before loading a role profile. Existing legacy history remains stored; internal agency role chats retain their existing entry point.
- App-owned Growth Partner memory is tenant-scoped, reviewable by authorized workspace members, and editable/removable by owners/admins with revision checks, duplicate prevention, and metadata-only audits. Explicit Remember:/Forget: commands write or remove memory; relevant notes enter prompts as untrusted context. Commands remain in the visible transcript but are excluded from later model history. This app database memory is separate from Hermes-native memory.
- Chat actions use existing workspace services. Coverage includes duplicate-safe CRM contact creation, private calendar and appointment-type creation, booking-rule updates, scheduled-job changes reflected on the Work Board, calendar availability, and approval-gated appointment booking.

It updates the same schedule, preserves its research assignment, recurrence days and enabled/paused state, and schedules one run at the window start.
- Opportunity-stage changes use the shared CRM tool service. A Growth Partner-specific test verifies the stage stays unchanged while approval is pending, then changes with an audit record after approval. The client tool policy exposes CRM writes only in its proposals phase.
- First-party attribution stores website form/chat UTM and referrer data, distinguishes first and latest touch, and reports recorded appointments, deals, and verified invoice revenue. Tests keep unknown or missing attribution and revenue explicit.
- Product language and entry points were updated across onboarding, calendar, leads, chat, Work Board, demo, purchase pages, CRM workflow summaries, login, Integrations health, and legacy-profile navigation. Demo copy now describes one AI Growth Partner identity with six retained capabilities; legacy employee records and workflows remain available during staged cutover.

- Client detail pages for retained roles now identify the record as a previous employee profile, explain that saved settings, schedules and history remain available, and route new requests to the permanent AI Growth Partner conversation. Enabled legacy entries on the client overview also open that canonical conversation; disabled records remain reviewable. Super Admin internal role-chat links are unchanged.

It creates one runtime identity and generates a non-empty, structured partner configuration from confirmed communication preferences, goals, ideal customers, and protected operating rules. Its control center links to existing workspace controls, filtered by the signed-in member's permissions.

Only bounded onboarding choices are used to shape this persona; permissions and approval gates remain authoritative.
- Confirmed onboarding communication preferences now reach the Growth Partner's versioned runtime settings: tone, reply length, language, company rules, prohibited promises, handoff conditions, goals, customer fit, and the requested answer/book, answer/handoff, or draft-only interaction mode. The interaction mode is stored as a preference and cannot grant tools or bypass approvals. Resubmissions synchronize only fields still owned by onboarding; client-written personality and settings are preserved, and no-op submissions do not report an unchanged partner as newly processed.

Unrelated Brain facts and pricing are preserved; a separate newer Brain draft is rebased but stays a draft, and instruction-like changed text is held for review instead of entering the active Brain. The save response and audit distinguish applied, review-held, and failed Growth Partner updates. No schema change was needed.
- Company-wide AI preference settings now include an enabled, configured Growth Partner runtime as well as retained profiles when an owner/admin explicitly turns on “Apply.” The preview, client-edit preservation, review gate, and isolated per-profile failures apply to the partner too; default-off saves still update shared defaults without changing any runtime version. Settings now name the Growth Partner in the control and success message. A database-backed test covers a Growth Partner-only workspace, the no-apply path, preview and applied version, plus isolation from a second tenant.
- The permanent Growth Partner conversation now supports explicit, persistent tone, reply-length, and language changes. Skipped, unknown, or unconfirmed answers remain excluded or visibly draft; file references and visual assets stay in their asset and knowledge systems rather than becoming employee instructions.

The pinned image has now been built and exercised in hosted acceptance run #87 using only the local model fixture; live discovery remains untested.
